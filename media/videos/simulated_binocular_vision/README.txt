
Simulated Binocular Vision Videos of Healthy Individuals and Strabismus Patient

This folder contains videos generated from the robotic eye platform to simulate and visualize binocular vision for healthy individuals and a strabismus patient. These videos were used in the study:

"A Novel Cable-driven Eye Robot For Human Vision Visualization and Strabismus Diagnosis"

Overview

The videos visually demonstrate the effects of normal and impaired binocular vision using simulated eye movement data. They are used to illustrate how strabismus impacts visual perception.

healthy people vision.avi

- Simulated binocular view for healthy individuals
- Generated using eye movement data from healthy participants
- Visual features:
  - Targets appear fused and clear
  - Peripheral vision blurred to mimic human foveation and focus
- Purpose:
  - Represents normal binocular fusion as experienced by healthy people

Strabismus patient vision.avi

- Simulated binocular view for a strabismus patient
- Generated using eye movement data from clinical strabismus cases
- Visual features:
  - Targets appear duplicated or misaligned (diplopia effect)
  - Simulates visual confusion and impairment typical of strabismus
- Purpose:
  - Demonstrates visual dysfunction due to strabismus for research and educational use

Usage

These videos are intended to be used for:
- Supplementary material in scientific publications
- Clinical and educational demonstrations of healthy vs. strabismic binocular vision
- Validation of the robotic eye platform's ability to replicate human visual experience
