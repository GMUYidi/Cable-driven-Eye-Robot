
Test and Calibration Videos for Eye Gaze Data Collection and Robotic Eye Positioning

This folder contains two videos used for human eye gaze data collection and robotic eye platform calibration. These videos are essential for ensuring accurate mapping between human gaze behavior and robotic eye motion simulation.

Overview of Videos

1️⃣ test_chessboard_60.avi

Purpose: Eye gaze data collection
Description:
This video was shown to human participants during the eye tracking experiments. Participants were instructed to watch the video while an eye tracker recorded their eye movements in real time. The recorded data includes eye gaze direction (yaw and pitch angles), which are later used to drive the robotic eye platform and simulate natural eye movements.
Usage:
- Visual stimulus for human eye tracking
- Source for generating gaze trajectory and angle datasets

2️⃣ calibration_chessboard_60.avi

Purpose: Robotic eye platform positioning and calibration
Description:
This video was used to calibrate the robotic eye platform before the experiment. By playing the video on a monitor and adjusting the robotic eye's physical position until the platform's gaze direction aligned with the chessboard pattern shown in the video, the platform was positioned to accurately mimic the human eye's location and perspective during the eye tracking experiment.
Usage:
- Calibration reference for aligning robotic eye position
- Ensures consistency between human and robotic eye viewpoints
