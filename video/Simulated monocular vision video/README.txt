
Normal Binocular Video Recordings (Left and Right Eye Views)

This folder contains left and right eye video recordings of a healthy individual watching a visual stimulus. The videos were used for generating binocular fused views and validating the robotic eye platform's ability to simulate normal vision.

These videos are part of the study:

"A Novel Cable-driven Eye Robot For Human Vision Visualization and Strabismus Diagnosis"

Overview

The videos were captured using a binocular eye tracking setup, with synchronized left and right eye cameras. The recordings represent natural eye movement and visual input of a healthy individual.

normal video left 2024.avi

- Left eye view of the visual stimulus during the experiment
- Shows the visual field and fixation behavior from the left eye perspective

normal video right 2024.avi

- Right eye view of the visual stimulus during the experiment
- Shows the visual field and fixation behavior from the right eye perspective

Usage

These videos are intended to be used for:
- Generating binocular fusion images
- Validating robotic eye simulation accuracy for healthy vision
- Serving as baseline reference for comparison with strabismus patient vision simulations
