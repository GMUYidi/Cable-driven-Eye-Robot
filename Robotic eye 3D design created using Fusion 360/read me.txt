Robotic Eye 3D Printable Model (STL Files)
This repository contains the 3D design models (STL format) of a robotic eyeball platform designed using AutoDesk Fusion 360 and fabricated via PLA 3D printing. These models are part of the study:

"A Novel Cable-driven Eye Robot For Human Vision Visualization and Strabismus Diagnosis"

The models enable the construction of a robotic eyeball capable of integrating sensors such as the BNO055 IMU, supporting precise anatomical replication and experimental studies.

Overview of Components
The robotic eyeball model consists of the following printable parts:

Main base of left eye

Main base of right eye

Front eye support of left eye

Front eye support of right eye

Front half of the eyeball adapted for the BNO055 IMU

Rear half of the eyeball adapted for the BNO055 IMU

Bolt (for assembly purposes)

These parts are designed as modular components to facilitate easy assembly, maintenance, and integration of electronics such as IMUs and camera modules.

Design Software
AutoDesk Fusion 360
The models were designed using Fusion 360 and exported as STL files for 3D printing.

Fabrication Method
Material: PLA (Polylactic Acid)

Printer Type: Standard FDM 3D printer

Recommended Print Settings:

Layer height: 0.2 mm

Infill: 20–40%

Supports: Required for overhanging parts (especially eye halves)

Orientation: For optimal surface quality and strength, follow the Fusion 360 design orientation.

Assembly
The front half and rear half of the eyeball are designed to enclose the BNO055 IMU securely.

The main base and eye supports hold the eyeball while allowing controlled rotation via cable-driven mechanisms.

Bolts are provided to secure parts during assembly.

Usage
The printed robotic eye model can be used for:

Replicating human eye movement with anatomical realism

Integrating sensors for eye motion tracking (IMU, camera)

Experimental validation of robotic eye control strategies

Visual demonstration and education purposes
